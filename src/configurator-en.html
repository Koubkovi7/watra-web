      How it works

      From measurements
      to your sauna.

      1. Measure the sauna inside.
        Width, depth and height from floor to ceiling. For a planned sauna, the dimensions from the design are enough.
      2. Place the heater and benches.
        The heater usually stands by a wall near the door, with the benches opposite. The golden zone shows where nothing combustible should be.
      3. Walk around and sit down.
        In 3D you will see whether there is enough room to load wood and how close to the bench the heater will be.

      Questions and answers

      Before you start building.

      How large a sauna can an IRIKON heat?+

      We will set the exact volume range once certification is complete. For now, the configurator uses the typical range of wood-fired heaters with a similar output. Each m² of glass or uninsulated wall counts as if the sauna had 1.2 m³ more volume.

      How far must the heater be from the wall and the bench?+

      For now we count on about 16 cm from the walls. A heat shield is a steel panel on a side or the back of the heater (accessory, CZK 1,000 each, up to 3): with it, about half is enough on that side, so the heater can stand closer to the wall. The full distance applies to benches and in front of the door. Certification will confirm the exact values and they will be in the installation manual.

      What do I need for the chimney?+

      The heater’s flue outlet is 115 mm in diameter and points up. The connection to the chimney, the ceiling pass-through and the flue inspection are handled by a chimney sweep according to local regulations.

      What does the electric part of the IRIKON +e need?+

      The electric part has 3 × 3 kW and needs a three-phase 400 V supply and an external sauna controller. A 9 kW heater is usually protected by a 3 × 16 A breaker; an electrician will design the protection and wiring. Electricity alone usually heats a sauna of up to 9 m³.

      I have a wooden floor. What goes under the heater?+

      A wood-fired heater needs a non-combustible base that extends in front of the door and to the sides, so that no hot ember lands on wood. With stones the heater weighs about 150 kg, and the floor must carry it.

      Do I need a chimney inspection?+

      Yes. Before the first firing, a chimney sweep must inspect the flue, and then clean and check it regularly according to local regulations.

      How should the sauna be ventilated?+

      The configurator places three vents: a combustion air inlet behind the heater about 35 cm above the floor, a circulation vent on the opposite wall at about 60 cm by the door, and a closable vent diagonally opposite the heater near the ceiling for airing after the sauna or when it gets too hot. We will confirm the exact placement for the IRIKON with the installation manual.
